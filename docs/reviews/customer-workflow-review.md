## 4. Overall verdict

**No, not ready to build the database contract unchanged.** The core customer journey is coherent, but first settle **what one unit means** and **which price a reservation promises**. Record expiry eligibility and retry behavior while designing the stock functions.

Then proceed with B1. Keep the 30-minute hold, one-shop basket and code-based pickup for the demo; fit the small wording/navigation improvements into wiring. There is no evidence here to justify extending the timer or adding the excluded features.
